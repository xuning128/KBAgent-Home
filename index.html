穿戴裝置如何辨識心房顫動How wearables detect atrial fibrillation

研究論文Research paper · 2026.11.10

科別與標題

心臟內科｜Detection of Atrial Fibrillation in a Large Population Using Wearable Devices: The Fitbit Heart Study
前瞻性、單臂、遠距研究
2020年收案，2022年發表

常見疾病與影響族群

心房顫動（AF）、心房撲動
相關臨床問題包含中風與心衰竭
研究納入美國年滿22歲、持有相容 Fitbit 與智慧型手機、自述未診斷 AF 的成人
中位年齡47歲，女性71%，白人73%

疾病如何診斷

心房顫動須由醫師判讀心電圖（ECG）確認，可使用十二導程心電圖或可判讀的心電監測紀錄
穿戴裝置的光學脈搏（PPG）通知可提示進一步檢查，Fitbit Heart Study 以心電圖貼片追蹤確認
ACC／AHA 心房顫動指引 ↗

是否需要長期回診

確診心房顫動後通常需要長期追蹤症狀、中風與出血風險，以及用藥是否合適
只有手錶通知時，先由醫師評估是否要用心電圖確認
ACC／AHA 心房顫動指引 ↗

本週篇文章重點

  • 規模與通知：455,699人中，4,728人收到不規則心律通知（約1.0%）
    風險觀察時間中位數122天
    通知率不等於 AF 盛行率
  • 演算法門檻：PPG 每2.5分鐘產生一段5分鐘脈搏資料，連續11段不規則才觸發 IHRD，等於至少30分鐘
    只分析靜止時段，可能漏掉短暫或活動時發作
  • 兩個分母：通知後可分析的1,057份 ECG 貼片中，340份發現 AF（32.2%）
    貼片監測時再出現 IHRD 且可判讀的225人中，221人同時有 AF，PPV 為98.2%（95% CI 95.5–99.5%）
  • 研究限制：探索性貼片分析的敏感度67.6%、特異度98.4%，適用該已篩選樣本
    研究有追蹤流失，且 Fitbit 出資並參與
    尚未證明此篩檢降低中風或死亡

關鍵名詞

AF：atrial fibrillation
PPG：photoplethysmography
IHRD：irregular heart rhythm detection
ECG：electrocardiogram
PPV：positive predictive value

討論

  • 為什麼要討論

    消費型穿戴裝置把心律偵測帶入日常，需理解通知後的確認流程

  • 為什麼重要

    98.2% 不能解讀為所有使用者的診斷準確率
    沒有通知也不能排除 AF
    最佳後續監測時間與臨床效益仍待確認

來源與定位

本地 PDF 共10頁
摘要與方法見PDF第1–3頁，主要數據第5頁，限制與探索性分析第9頁
原始標題與 DOI 見來源

Scope and population

Cardiology. This prospective single-arm remote study enrolled US Fitbit users aged at least 22 years without self-reported AF. There were 455,699 participants; median age was 47, 71% were female and 73% were White. Relevant diseases include AF and atrial flutter, with stroke and heart failure as related concerns.

Diagnosis

A clinician confirms atrial fibrillation by reviewing an ECG tracing, such as a 12-lead ECG or an interpretable ambulatory ECG recording. An optical pulse (PPG) alert can prompt testing. The Fitbit Heart Study used ECG patches for follow-up.
ACC/AHA atrial fibrillation guideline ↗

Long-term follow-up

Confirmed atrial fibrillation usually calls for ongoing review of symptoms, stroke and bleeding risk, and medication. A wearable alert calls for assessment of whether ECG confirmation is needed.
ACC/AHA atrial fibrillation guideline ↗

Key findings

  • Notifications occurred in 4,728 participants (about 1.0%) over a median 122 days at risk. Notification frequency is not AF prevalence.
  • The PPG algorithm required 11 consecutive overlapping five-minute irregular tachograms, representing at least 30 minutes, during inactivity. Short or active episodes may be missed.
  • AF was found in 340 of 1,057 subsequent analyzable ECG patches (32.2%). Concurrent AF occurred in 221 of 225 evaluable participants with another IHRD during ECG monitoring: PPV 98.2% (95% CI 95.5–99.5%). These denominators answer different questions.
  • Exploratory sensitivity was 67.6% and specificity 98.4% in the selected patch sample. Attrition, population selection and Fitbit funding limit interpretation; stroke and mortality benefits were not established.

Keywords

AF, PPG, IHRD, ECG, PPV: atrial fibrillation, photoplethysmography, irregular heart rhythm detection, electrocardiogram, positive predictive value.

Discussion

  • Why discuss it

    wearable notifications require a clinical confirmation pathway.

  • Why it matters

    a high conditional PPV is not population-wide accuracy, and no notification does not rule out AF. Optimal follow-up and clinical benefit remain uncertain.

Scope and population

Oncology and geriatric medicine. This 36-page supplementary appendix examines facial age estimates and overall survival. The local file does not include the main article. Three retrospective cancer cohorts total 6,196 patients: MAASTRO 4,906, Harvard Thoracic 573 and Harvard Palliative 717. The 535 controls include benign conditions and DCIS; they are not uniformly healthy volunteers.

Diagnosis

The diagnostic workup depends on the cancer type. It can include medical history, examination, imaging and lab tests. Many cancers require a biopsy for confirmation. The FaceAge study explored prognosis in people already diagnosed with cancer.
NCI cancer diagnosis ↗ · FaceAge research project ↗

Long-term follow-up

Cancer care usually includes follow-up after treatment. Cancer type, stage, treatment and individual circumstances guide the schedule and tests. The FaceAge study does not establish a follow-up schedule.
NCI follow-up after cancer treatment ↗

Key findings

  • Training used 56,304 curated IMDB-Wiki photographs; independent UTK technical validation included 2,547 people. No clinical patient data were used for training. Chronological age labels are not a biological-age reference standard.
  • MTCNN face extraction precedes an Inception-ResNet v1 encoder and 128-dimensional embedding. Excluding poor-quality and obscured images limits application to unfiltered clinical photographs.
  • Cancer patients had a mean FaceAge minus chronological-age difference of 4.79 years. In adjusted MAASTRO analysis, the mortality HR per FaceAge decade was 1.151 (95% CI 1.057–1.254). Association does not establish causality or an individual lifespan.
  • Table 5 reports a three-year survival AUC of about 0.615 (95% CI 0.595–0.634). Several comparator differences were nonsignificant. The 100-case survey involved ten raters, including two lay researchers.

Keywords

FaceAge, biological age, chronological age, MTCNN, CNN, overall survival, hazard ratio, AUC, ECOG, TEACHH.

Discussion

  • Why discuss it

    photographs may contribute prognostic information beyond age.

  • Why it matters

    calibration, fairness and prospective utility require validation before treatment decisions. Applicability to Taiwan remains unconfirmed.

Scope and population

Cardiology. Hypertension and related cardiovascular and kidney disease affect adults, including younger people.

Diagnosis

Clinicians assess whether blood pressure is consistently elevated using repeated standardized measurements. The 2022 Taiwan guideline uses the 722 home protocol and excludes day one from the average. An average systolic pressure of at least 130 mmHg or diastolic pressure of at least 80 mmHg meets its threshold for hypertension.
Taiwan hypertension guideline ↗

Long-term follow-up

Confirmed hypertension usually requires ongoing follow-up. Home 722 readings inform review, and the visit interval depends on blood pressure control, medication changes and other conditions.
Taiwan hypertension guideline ↗

Key findings

  • Symptoms can be absent; regular measurement matters.
  • Use seven days, morning and evening, two readings per session.
  • Rest first and maintain quiet, consistent posture.

Keywords

Hypertension; home blood pressure monitoring; white-coat hypertension.

Discussion

  • Why discuss it

    symptoms may miss risk.

  • Why it matters

    reliable records support evaluation.

  • HPA verification

    722 gives 28 raw readings across 14 sessions. Follow-up frequency is individualized; historical article statistics are not current estimates.

Calcium and vitamin D support bone health. These notes cover dietary sources and assessment of bone density and fracture risk.

Specialty

Nutrition

Related conditions

Postmenopausal osteoporosis and fragility fractures.

Diagnosis

In postmenopausal women, a DXA T-score of −2.5 or lower at an eligible hip or lumbar spine site meets a diagnostic criterion. A low-trauma hip or vertebral fracture can also establish osteoporosis.

Long-term follow-up

Diagnosed osteoporosis generally requires ongoing review of fracture risk and treatment response. BHOF advises bone density testing about one to two years after starting or changing medication, with later intervals guided by risk.

Population

Postmenopausal women, especially those of older age or with a previous low-trauma fracture.

Key points

  • Compare calcium per serving. Dairy, fish eaten with their bones and some leafy greens provide calcium. Convert per-100-gram values to the serving actually eaten.
  • Identify vitamin D sources. Fatty fish, egg yolks and some fortified foods provide vitamin D, which helps the intestine absorb calcium.
  • Separate nutrition from assessment. Bone density and fracture history help assess osteoporosis. Dietary intake helps determine whether nutrition is adequate.
  • Include nutrition in fracture prevention. Nutrition can be combined with weight-bearing activity, fall prevention and osteoporosis treatment when indicated.

Key terms

DXA (dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry), BMD (bone mineral density), T-score and fragility fracture.

Discussion

  • Why discuss it

    Usual serving sizes give a more practical estimate of calcium and vitamin D intake.

  • Why it matters

    Fracture risk informs follow-up and treatment. The applicability of the article’s intake figures to specific age groups and regions remains unconfirmed.
